Choosing AP courses is confusing. Let me help.

I made this site because figuring out which APs to take shouldn't be this hard.

Real talk: The College Board site tells you what's IN each AP course, but not which ones you actually need for your major or career. That's where this comes in.

So here's the deal: You can browse by your intended major (if you know what you want to study in college) or by career (if you know what job you're aiming for). Either way works.

I've mapped out which AP courses actually matter for different paths based on College Board data and what colleges typically require. Plus, I'll tell you which ones might get you college credit so you can save time and money.

Why I made this

When I was picking my AP courses, I had no idea what I was doing. My counselor was helpful but overworked. The College Board site was overwhelming. I just wanted someone to tell me: "If you're thinking about engineering, take these APs. Here's why."

So I built that resource. This site shows you:

  • Which AP courses are actually relevant for your goals
  • Why each course matters (or doesn't)
  • Which ones typically give you college credit
  • No BS, just helpful info from one student to another

One important note: Every college has different AP credit policies. The info here is based on common patterns, but definitely check with your specific schools to see what they accept.

How to use this site

It's pretty simple:

  1. Click on either "By Major" or "By Career" above
  2. Find your field of interest
  3. See which AP courses are recommended
  4. Use that info to plan your schedule (and talk to your counselor)
